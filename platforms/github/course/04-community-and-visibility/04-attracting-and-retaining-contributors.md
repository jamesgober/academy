# Attracting and Retaining Contributors

> Contributors stay where expectations are clear and maintainers are responsive.

**You will learn:**
- What attracts first-time contributors
- How maintainers can reduce contributor drop-off
- Which signals make a repository feel healthy

**Before this page, you should know:** [Gists and Sharing Small Knowledge](./03-gists-and-sharing-small-knowledge.md)

---

## Attract contributors

- Keep README clear and current.
- Label beginner tasks (`good first issue`).
- Write issue templates with clear reproduction fields.

## Retain contributors

- Respond quickly and respectfully.
- Review PRs with actionable feedback.
- Thank contributors publicly in release notes.

## Health signals people notice

- frequent commits
- updated docs
- maintained issue queue
- predictable release cadence

> [!TIP]
> The fastest way to lose contributors is silence after they open a first PR.

---

## Recap

- Contributor growth depends on documentation and maintainership behavior.
- Labels and templates lower contribution friction.
- Response quality strongly affects community trust.

## Try it yourself

Create a `good first issue` with clear acceptance criteria and a short context
section.
